Lots/trade
Every strike is listed that passes the hard gates (Trend, Delta band, Reach, Liquidity, Event gate) and misses no more than your tolerance among the 17 checks (Strict = all 17; hover a dot for its name). Technical (5): EMA20/50 trend · RSI zone · MACD momentum · ADX≥20 with DI agreement · Bollinger position. Options (8): Delta band · Gamma ≥ median · Theta ≤5%/day · IV percentile ≤60 · OI change ≥+5% · Reach (1.5×ATR can deliver your R:R) · Liquidity (spread/volume/OI, real via Angel One) · Edge (Win% ≥ break-even+3pts). Fundamental (3): no earnings/event before expiry · sector & market-breadth strength · growth/P-E quality (from your Settings data; blank = n/a, neutral). Then Score ≥ 60. Win% = drift-adjusted chance premium hits target before stop; EV(R) = Win%×R − (1−Win%). Heuristics, not guarantees. "~" after OI Δ = modelled. Event mode relaxes ADX/Bollinger/IV and uses confirmed-move rules.
Closed trades are retained (browser storage) until cleared.
Fees per trade: brokerage ₹20×2, STT on sell premium, exchange txn, SEBI, stamp duty (buy), GST 18% on brokerage+txn+SEBI. Rates are editable in CFG at the top of the script — verify against your broker's current contract note. Open-position net P&L includes estimated exit fees.

Fundamentals & event calendar
One line each. Stock: SYMBOL, earnings date, P/E, revenue growth %, EPS growth % (blank if unknown). Event: EVENT, date, label. Saved only in this browser. A strike is vetoed if earnings or an event falls before its expiry (unless Event mode is on). Blank data = "n/a" (neutral, not a pass or fail).
