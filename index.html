Criteria (all must pass): Trend (price/EMA20/EMA50) · RSI zone · Delta 0.30–0.60 · Gamma ≥ chain median · Theta ≤ 5%/day of premium · IV percentile ≤ 60 · OI change ≥ +5% · Reach (a 1.5×ATR move must be able to deliver your chosen R:R) · Liquidity (spread ≤4%, volume, OI — real once Angel One is connected) · Score ≥ 60. Win% is a drift-adjusted barrier-hit estimate (target before stop), a heuristic and not a guarantee. "~" after OI Δ = modelled, not real. Educational tool — not investment advice.
Closed trades are retained (browser storage) until cleared.
Fees per trade: brokerage ₹20×2, STT on sell premium, exchange txn, SEBI, stamp duty (buy), GST 18% on brokerage+txn+SEBI. Rates are editable in CFG at the top of the script — verify against your broker's current contract note. Open-position net P&L includes estimated exit fees.
